Frequently Asked Questions

How soon will I see progress?

Most members notice better energy and consistency within a few weeks; strength gains vary by program and experience.

I'm new to lifting—is that okay?

Yes. Coaches teach fundamentals first, then progress load and volume at a pace that fits you.

Is equipment inspected and are coaches certified?

Yes. We keep equipment inspected, floors clean, and coaches certified in first aid and CPR.

Do you offer trials or day passes?

Yes—ask the front desk for a day pass or intro session so you can experience the gym before you commit.
