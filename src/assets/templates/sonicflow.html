FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions before you visit

Do you offer a trial or day pass?
Yes—ask the front desk for a guest pass or short trial so you can train during peak hours and meet a coach.
Can I freeze or cancel my membership?
We offer flexible holds for travel or injury and straightforward cancellation terms—details are in your agreement at signup.
Are lockers and showers included?
Day-use lockers and showers are included. Long-term locker rental may be available depending on location.
Do you have personal training?
Yes—add one-on-one or small-group PT blocks to any membership. Coaches build plans around your schedule and goals.
What are peak hours?
Weekday mornings and early evenings are busiest. We stagger class start times to keep the floor moving smoothly.
Is there parking?
Street and garage spots nearby; bike racks are at the main entrance. Ask staff for the best option on your visit.

Ready to train with us?

Book a tour or start a trial week—we will match you with the right membership.
